About Us

Kimtech Geomatics is one of Canada’s largest geomatics firms, providing land surveying, mapping, forestry, environmental, and geospatial solutions across the country. With roots in the industry that stretch back over 100 years, we continue to evolve and position ourselves for a rapidly-changing future, while remaining true to the founding principle on which the company was built: to provide outstanding service from down-to-earth people.
